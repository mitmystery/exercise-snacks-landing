Brand Summary

What is Exercise Snacks?

Exercise Snacks: Desk Breaks is an iOS mobile app published by Mithul Mistry (bundle ID: com.mithulmistry.exercisesnacks) that helps office workers interrupt prolonged sitting with brief, guided movement breaks — called "exercise snacks" — throughout the workday.

The app is grounded in peer-reviewed research demonstrating that short bouts of bodyweight exercise performed frequently during the day — particularly bodyweight squats — produce measurable metabolic, cardiovascular, and cognitive benefits comparable to or exceeding traditional exercise patterns.

The product philosophy is: movement should feel like a treat, not a chore. The design is warm, illustrated, and compassionate — closer to Headspace than a gym tracker.

Core Product

What the app does

Exercise Snacks prompts users to take short movement breaks at configurable intervals throughout their workday, guides them through a bodyweight exercise, and tracks their progress over time.

Smart Break Reminders. Three notification modes — Focus (every 45 min), Balanced (every 30 min), and Active (every 20 min) — let users match break frequency to their work style. Reminders are local and privacy-respecting; no account required.

30-Exercise Guided Library. Exercises span four categories: Strength, Stretch, Cardio, and Balance. The hero exercise is the bodyweight squat, supported by dedicated UI treatments, science callouts, and specific progress tracking. 8 exercises are free; 22 are Premium.

Compassionate Progress Tracking. A flexible streak system, snack counter, and milestone badges celebrate consistency without punishing misses. Any completed exercise snack counts toward the daily goal — no guilt-driven mechanics.

Lottie Animations. Each exercise is demonstrated through custom Lottie animations (not stock video), keeping the app lightweight, brand-consistent, and accessible without licensing concerns.

The Science

Research behind the app

The "exercise snack" concept — brief vigorous bouts under 5 minutes, repeated throughout the day — has moved from niche curiosity to mainstream exercise science. The app's core recommendation (10 bodyweight squats every 45 minutes) is based on a direct randomized trial.

21%
Reduction in postprandial blood glucose from 10 squats every 45 minutes — identical to frequent walking breaks
Gao et al. (2024), Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports
39%
Glucose reduction from resistance activity breaks (half-squats, calf raises) every 30 minutes in adults with type 2 diabetes
Dempsey et al. (2016), Diabetes Care
29%
Increase in muscle protein synthesis from bodyweight squats every 30 minutes during 7.5 hours of sitting
Moore et al. (2022), Journal of Applied Physiology
~40%
Reduction in all-cause and cancer mortality from just 3 bouts per day of vigorous activity lasting 1–2 minutes each
Stamatakis et al. (2022), Nature Medicine (UK Biobank, n=25,241)
3.5%
Faster reaction times maintained with 1-minute squat breaks every 20 minutes vs. progressive cognitive decline in sedentary group
Horiuchi et al., desk work cognition study
27
Studies pooled in a 2025 meta-analysis confirming exercise snacks improved VO₂max, blood pressure, glucose, HDL, and LDL
Frontiers in Cardiovascular Medicine (2025), n=970 participants

Key finding: Quadriceps EMG amplitude is the strongest predictor of glucose clearance, meaning large-muscle contraction — regardless of whether from walking or squatting — drives the metabolic benefit. Squats additionally offer triglyceride reduction, anabolic signalling, and cognitive improvements that walking does not match.

Differentiation

How Exercise Snacks differs

Science-first, not motivation-first. Most desk break apps are simple timers. Exercise Snacks is built on peer-reviewed evidence — specifically a 2024 randomized trial (Gao et al.) — with the science surfaced in-app rather than buried in a blog post.

Squats as the hero, not steps. Walking-based break apps require leaving a desk. Bodyweight squats deliver equivalent glycemic benefit, plus additional triglyceride reduction and muscle protein synthesis, in under 30 seconds at your chair.

Compassionate gamification. Inspired by Gentler Streak's Apple Design Award-winning approach — rest days don't break streaks, the app celebrates any movement, and progress is compared only to your past self. No guilt mechanics.

Warm, illustrated design. Custom Lottie animations and a coral + sage green palette inspired by Headspace's approachable aesthetic. Avoids the gym-style dark UI and aggressive typography common in fitness apps.

Priced fairly. At £49.99/year, Exercise Snacks significantly undercuts Wakeout ($99/year) and Moova ($59.99/year) while offering a generous free tier with 8 exercises and unlimited reminders.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Q Does Exercise Snacks really help with blood sugar?
Yes, based on direct clinical evidence. A 2024 randomized crossover trial (Gao et al.) found that 10 bodyweight squats every 45 minutes reduced postprandial blood glucose by approximately 21% — identical to the effect of frequent walking breaks and nearly double the benefit of a single 30-minute walk. The mechanism is large-muscle contraction pulling glucose from the bloodstream.
Q Do I need any equipment?
No. Every exercise in the library is bodyweight-only and designed to be performed in a standard office, home desk, or standing position. No gym, no mat, no shoes required.
Q How long does each break take?
Each exercise snack takes under 2 minutes. The hero exercise — 10 bodyweight squats — takes roughly 30 seconds. The total daily time investment for a full Balanced schedule is approximately 5–10 minutes across an 8-hour workday.
Q How much does it cost?
The app is free to download with 8 exercises included at no cost. The Premium Annual subscription is £49.99/year and unlocks all 30 exercises plus advanced tracking features. There is no monthly pricing option currently.
Q Is Exercise Snacks available on Android?
Currently iOS only. The app is built with Expo (React Native), which supports Android, and Android availability may come in a future release.
Q What makes this different from a simple timer app?
Timer apps remind you to stand; Exercise Snacks guides you through specific, science-backed exercises with animated demonstrations, tracks your progress, and explains the research behind each movement. The bodyweight squat is promoted not arbitrarily but because a peer-reviewed trial demonstrated its equivalence to walking for glucose control.
Q Who makes Exercise Snacks?
Exercise Snacks: Desk Breaks is an independent iOS app published by Mithul Mistry. The app is available on the Apple App Store.
